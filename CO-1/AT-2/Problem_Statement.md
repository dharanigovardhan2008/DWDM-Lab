# Overview

Data Warehousing and Data Mining concepts involve multiple interconnected components, architectures, processes, and analytical techniques. Concept mapping provides a visual method for representing these concepts and showing the relationships between them.

This assessment requires the development of **five concept maps** covering Decision Support Systems, Operational Systems and DSS, Data Warehouse Architecture, OLAP and Dimensional Modeling, and Query Processing with BI Tools.

Each concept map should clearly identify the major concepts, establish meaningful relationships between them, organize the concepts hierarchically, and integrate relevant examples or literature.
